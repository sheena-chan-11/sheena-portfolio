About Me

It's Sheena here! 👋

I have a background in digital campaigns, helping nonprofits raise money through email campaigns. From the start of my career, I’ve wanted to combine technical skills with creativity in my work. Along the way, I discovered the world of human-centered design.

My UX adventure started back in 2011 when I joined a 12-week UX Bootcamp in Hong Kong, and I’ve wanted to dive deeper ever since. Now, I’m pursuing a master’s degree in Human-Centered Design & Engineering at the University of Washington to take my skills to the next level!

When I’m not working or studying, I love getting out into nature through camping and hiking to reset my mind. 🏕️ And guess what? I’ve been babysitting my sourdough starter! 🍞EmailLinkedIn ↗
